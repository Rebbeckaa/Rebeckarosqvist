January 2025 · Web Design · E-commerce / Concept

cool fashion

A bold, rule-breaking e-commerce concept for a fashion brand that dares to be different.

Overview

Cool Fashion is a self-initiated web design concept for an edgy fashion e-commerce brand. The direction is unapologetically bold — a dark, high-contrast interface built around a single statement: dare to be different.

The look

A dramatic full-bleed hero with a glitch-style headline, set against deep black and stark white type. The aesthetic is confident and a little rebellious — made for a fashion-forward audience that wants to stand out.

What I explored

I designed the core shopping experience — landing page, product listing, and cart — focusing on a cohesive visual language and a clear path from browsing to checkout.
